Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my game saved?

Your colony, isopods and Field Guide are saved on your device and in your own iCloud — no account needed. Reinstall the App or open it on a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple ID, and your game comes back, items and purchase record included. If iCloud is off for the App (iOS Settings → your name → iCloud), the game is saved on the device only and deleting the App deletes it.

What happens while I'm away?

The colony keeps growing and earning Compost for up to 8 hours (Research Notes can extend this). A pair in the breeding pen keeps hatching clutches too. When you come back, a report shows what happened.

How do I find new morphs?

Catch the sparkling mutants that visit the terrarium — feeding the colony brings the next one sooner. Each mutant carries one new gene. Most genes are hidden: they only show in a kid that gets two copies, so breed two carriers of the same gene. The Breeding Lab shows each pair's chance of a new find and suggests a good pair.

Why does my pair say there is nothing new to hatch?

Neither a new Field Guide entry nor a new trait can come from that pair yet — usually a second carrier of the gene is missing. Keep catching mutants or scoop isopods from the colony, then try the suggested pair.

What is Auto Breeding?

It opens after your first few Field Guide entries. Switch it on in the Breeding Lab and the pair in the pen keeps breeding, even while you're away; only finds (and kids a collector wants) stay in the hatchery, the rest join the colony. Switch it off whenever you want to choose each clutch yourself.

What does starting a new colony keep?

You keep your breeding slots, Field Guide, unlocked body types, Research Notes and the permanent bonus, which grows with each colony. Compost, upgrades and the colony itself start over, from founders you pick.

What do Ad-Free and Lab Pass include?

Ad-Free removes interstitial ads and adds 2 breeding slots. Lab Pass includes all of that, lets you claim ad rewards without watching ads, and raises Food Efficiency ×1.5. Ad-Free owners can upgrade to Lab Pass for the difference. Rewarded ads are always optional.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the menu, go to Store and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with. Ad-Free and Lab Pass come back on any device. Unused catalysts and rare food come back with your game through iCloud; items you used up can't be restored.

Why am I asked for my year of birth?

So the ads you may see suit your age. It's asked once, nothing is pre-selected, and the answer stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy for what changes for players under 13.

I find some isopod colors hard to tell apart.

Open Settings and set Isopod Colors to Color-Vision Assist. Body colors spread further apart for red-green and blue-yellow color blindness; genes and the Field Guide stay the same.

Troubleshooting

Most issues go away after closing the App from the app switcher and opening it again.
  • A purchase didn't unlock — Store → Restore Purchases, then reopen the App. Unlocks are checked with the App Store on every launch.
  • An ad button says to try again tomorrow — each reward has a daily limit; it resets at local midnight.
  • Ads don't load — check your internet connection. In some regions you are asked for ad consent first; you can review it under Settings → Privacy Choices.
  • No progress while away — away time counts up to the cap (8 hours at first).
  • The game feels slow or hot on an older iPhone — reopen the App; if it continues, please email us your device model.
