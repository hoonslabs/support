Frequently Asked Questions

Are my photos saved to iOS Photos?

No — never. OutVault completely bypasses the iOS Photos library. Every photo and video you capture is stored exclusively inside the app's private sandbox on your device. Nothing is written to your Camera Roll or Photos app.

Where exactly is my media stored?

Media files are stored in the app's Application Support/assets/ directory, protected by NSFileProtectionComplete — Apple's highest level of on-device encryption. Files are inaccessible while your device is locked. No other app or person can access these files without unlocking your device and passing Face ID / Passcode.

How does Face ID unlock work?

OutVault requires Face ID or your device Passcode every time you open the app, and again after it moves to the background. Authentication is handled entirely by iOS — the App never sees your biometric data. On the Simulator, authentication is automatically bypassed for development purposes.

How do I take a photo or record a video?

Tap the camera button in the gallery to open the camera. Tap the shutter to capture a photo — you'll be returned to the gallery immediately. Press and hold (or tap the record button) to start a video; tap again to stop. After recording, a "Video saved" toast appears and the camera stays open so you can record another clip right away.

How do I share a photo or video?

Open the asset in the detail view and tap the Share button. The standard iOS share sheet will appear. Pro users can strip all EXIF metadata (location, device, timestamp) before sharing — the share sheet will present a clean, metadata-free copy of the file.

How do I delete a photo or video?

Open the asset in the detail view and tap Delete, or long-press a thumbnail in the gallery and choose Delete from the context menu. Deletion is permanent — there is no trash or recycle bin.

What is Auto-Delete? (Pro)

Pro users can set an expiration date on any asset. Once the expiration time passes, the App automatically purges the file the next time you launch it, or via a background task. A local notification is sent in advance as a reminder. You control the expiration window individually per asset.

What is Metadata Editing? (Pro)

Pro users can edit the date, memo, author, and location text attached to any asset. The location field is free-form text — the App does not access GPS. Edited metadata is stored securely in the app sandbox alongside your media.

What is EXIF Strip? (Pro)

When you share a file, Pro users can choose to remove all embedded EXIF metadata (camera model, GPS coordinates, timestamps, etc.) before the file is handed to the iOS share sheet. The original file stored in your vault remains unchanged.

Will my data be deleted if I delete the app?

Yes. All media and metadata stored in the app sandbox is permanently deleted when you uninstall the App. There is no cloud backup unless you have CloudKit sync enabled (a future Pro feature). We recommend exporting important files via the share sheet before uninstalling.

How do I restore a purchase (Lifetime Pro)?

Go to Settings in the App and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase. Purchases are managed by RevenueCat and linked to your App Store account.

The app is showing the wrong date on my photos.

OutVault records the capture date at the moment the shutter fires. If the date appears wrong, check your device's date and time settings (Settings → General → Date & Time → Set Automatically). Pro users can also manually correct the date via Metadata Editing.

Can I use OutVault on the Simulator?

Yes. On the iOS Simulator, the camera is replaced by a color-tap UI that generates sample photos in six colors. Face ID is automatically bypassed. You can also seed 12 sample photos via Settings → Debug → Seed Sample Photos.

Troubleshooting

Most issues are resolved by force-closing the app and reopening it, or by restarting your iPhone.
  • Camera not opening / black screen — Go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera and make sure OutVault has camera permission enabled. Force-close and relaunch the app.
  • Face ID not prompting — Go to iOS Settings → Face ID & Passcode and confirm your Face ID is enrolled. Force-close the app and try again.
  • Gallery appears empty after reinstall — Media stored in the app sandbox is deleted with the app. If you had no CloudKit sync, data cannot be recovered. Export important files before uninstalling.
  • Auto-Delete notification not arriving — Go to iOS Settings → Notifications → OutVault and confirm notifications are allowed. The App maintains a single scheduled notification for the next expiring asset.
  • App is slow or freezing — Force-close the app (swipe up in the app switcher) and relaunch. Restart your iPhone if the issue persists.
  • Thumbnail not loading in gallery — Thumbnails are generated and cached on first display. Scroll away and back to retry, or force-close and relaunch.
